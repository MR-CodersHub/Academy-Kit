About This Kit

The Elite Pro Football Kit is engineered for school-level competitive play. Built on our signature 150gsm moisture-wicking polyester, this kit draws sweat away from the skin and dries 3× faster than cotton, keeping players cool and comfortable throughout match time.

The jersey features double-needle stitching at high-stress points, mesh underarm panels for ventilation, and a flatlock collar for comfort. Available in team-specific colorways with full sublimated printing — no peel, no crack.
