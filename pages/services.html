Materials

Our Fabric Options

Every fabric is tested for breathability, durability and colorfastness — so your kits look great all season long.

Moisture-Wicking Polyester

Our most popular choice. 100% polyester, 150gsm, pulls sweat away from skin for all-day comfort. Ideal for football & cricket.

Best For: Football, Cricket, Basketball

Breathable Mesh

Open-weave mesh fabric that allows maximum airflow. Ultra-lightweight at 80gsm. Perfect for athletics and warm-weather sports.

Best For: Athletics, Tennis, Swimming

Premium Cotton Blend

65% cotton, 35% polyester blend. Soft, breathable and hypoallergenic. Popular for cricket whites and school sports days.

Best For: Cricket, Sports Day, PE Wear

Recycled Polyester

Made from 100% recycled PET bottles. OEKO-TEX® certified, same performance as virgin polyester — but eco-friendly.

Best For: Eco-conscious schools

4-Way Stretch Spandex

90% polyester, 10% spandex. Maximum stretch and recovery. Ideal for gymnastics, athletics and yoga/PE uniform options.

Best For: Gymnastics, Athletics

Interlock Knit

Double-faced knit fabric, smooth both sides. Durable and wrinkle-resistant. Excellent for rugby, hockey & contact sports.

Best For: Rugby, Hockey, Netball
