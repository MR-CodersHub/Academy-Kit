How to Choose the Perfect Football Kit for Your School Team in 2026

By Arjun Mehta
September 28, 2026
8 min read
3.2K views

Choosing the right football kit for your school team is much more than picking colors and slapping a badge on a jersey. A great kit should perform under match conditions, fit every player comfortably, represent your school's identity, and survive the rigors of a full season — all without breaking your budget.

In this guide, we break down the key factors every PE coordinator and sports head should consider when ordering football kits in 2026.

1. Define Your Budget First

Before anything else, establish your per-unit budget. School football kits typically range from ₹799 for basic training jerseys to ₹2,500+ for full match kits with embroidered crests and player names. A realistic mid-range budget is ₹1,200–₹1,800 per player for a complete home-and-away set.

Pro Tip: Order in larger quantities to unlock bulk pricing discounts. Most suppliers (including AcademyKit) offer 10–25% price reductions for orders above 50 units.

2. Choose the Right Fabric

The most critical decision in kit selection is the fabric. For competitive school football, we strongly recommend:

  • Moisture-wicking polyester (150gsm) — The gold standard. Pulls sweat from the skin and dries fast. Essential for Indian climates.
  • Mesh panels — Underarm and back panels in open-weave mesh dramatically improve airflow during intensive play.
  • Avoid 100% cotton — Cotton absorbs sweat and becomes heavy, restricting movement and causing discomfort.
"A player wearing the right fabric is a player who can focus on the game — not on their discomfort."
— Arjun Mehta, Founder of AcademyKit

3. Sublimation vs. Screen Printing

For custom branding, you'll need to choose between sublimation printing and screen printing:

  • Sublimation printing — Colors are embedded into the fabric. No cracking, no peeling — ever. Best for complex designs and vivid colors. Available only on white base fabrics.
  • Screen printing — Works on any base color, less expensive for simple designs. Risk of cracking after 30+ washes.
  • Heat transfer — Good for names and numbers. Quick, cost-effective but not as durable as sublimation.

Our recommendation: For school kits that will be used 2–3 seasons, always choose sublimation. It costs marginally more upfront but saves significantly over the lifespan of the kit.

4. Size Range: Don't Get This Wrong

School football teams often have players aged 8–18 in the same squad. Order a full size run from junior to adult, and always order 10–15% extra in the most common sizes (typically S, M and L for seniors; 9-10y and 11-12y for juniors). Refer to our interactive size guide to ensure a proper athletic fit.

5. Customization Options

Beyond the team color and crest, consider these branding additions:

  • Embroidered school badge — Adds a premium, professional look. ₹49/badge.
  • Squad numbers — Essential for match play. Back and chest numbers available.
  • Player names — Great for tournaments and inter-school matches.
  • School name text — Across back collar or chest for team identity.

6. Lead Times and Delivery

Plan ahead. Custom kits take 7–14 business days to produce and deliver. For major inter-school tournaments or annual sports days, order at least 4 weeks in advance. Express production (5–7 days) is available at AcademyKit for urgent needs.

Quick Decision Checklist

  • Budget per player confirmed
  • Moisture-wicking fabric selected
  • Sublimation printing confirmed
  • Full size run with extras ordered
  • School crest file ready (SVG/AI)
  • Order placed 4+ weeks before event

Conclusion

A well-chosen football kit is an investment in your school's sports culture. It boosts team morale, creates a unified identity and, with the right fabric and printing, will serve your players for 2–3 seasons without fading or deteriorating. Follow these six steps and your school will be dressed to impress on every match day.
