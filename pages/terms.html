The rules for quotes, orders, delivery and returns.

Last updated: January 2026. By requesting a quote or placing an order with AcademyKit, you agree to these terms.

01

Quotations & Orders

Quotes are valid for 30 days. An order is confirmed only after written approval of the digital mockup, size chart and 50% advance payment (for orders above ₹50,000).

02

Custom & Branded Kits

Customised kits with your school crest, names or numbers are made to order and cannot be resold. Please verify spelling, colours and sizes on the mockup — approved errors cannot be reworked free of charge.

03

Pricing & Payment

Prices include basic sublimation printing; GST is charged extra as applicable. Balance payment is due before dispatch unless otherwise agreed in writing.

04

Production & Delivery

Standard production is 10–14 business days after approval; express 48-hour dispatch is available for urgencies. Delivery timelines shared at order time are estimates.

05

Returns & Exchanges

Unworn, uncustomised stock items can be exchanged within 30 days. Customised kits are eligible for remake only for manufacturing defects reported within 14 days of delivery.

Our liability is limited to the order value. These terms are governed by the laws of India, with jurisdiction in New Delhi.
