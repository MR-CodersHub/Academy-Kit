Who We Are

The Story Behind AcademyKit

Founded in 2009 by Arjun Mehta — a former national-level footballer and sports educator — AcademyKit began with a simple mission: give every school the same quality of sportswear that professional clubs enjoy, at prices schools can afford.

What started as a small Delhi workshop producing 500 football kits per season has grown into India's most trusted school sportswear partner — with state-of-the-art manufacturing in Ludhiana, Surat and Tiruppur, and a network serving 500+ schools across 28 states.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To make premium-quality sportswear accessible to every school in India, regardless of size or budget — fostering a culture of athletic pride and team spirit.

Our Vision

To become Asia's most innovative school sportswear brand — where cutting-edge design meets sustainable manufacturing and every kit tells a school's unique story.

Our Values

Quality over quantity. Partnership over transaction. Sustainability over shortcuts. We believe great sportswear inspires great athletes — and that is why we never compromise.
